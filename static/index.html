how to get your discord token
  1. Open discord.com in your browser and log in (not the desktop app).
  2. Press F12 to open DevTools and switch to the Network tab.
  3. Click any channel in Discord so the network tab fills with requests.
  4. In the filter box at the top of the Network tab, type /api.
  5. Click any matching request, then look at the Headers panel.
  6. Find authorization: in the request headers. The value next to it is your token.
  7. Right-click that value → Copy value, paste it above, click save.

Your token never leaves your browser except to the lurk proxy on its way to Discord. It's stored in this browser's localStorage.
